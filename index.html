Will attempt to get the location of your device to produce a clickable link to show your position on the map at maximum zoom level.


Enter a latitude and longitude in the dialogue boxes that pop up after hitting this button to produce a clickable link to show that place on the map at maximum zoom level.

...or browse the railway map below....

Red lines/names are ELRs (see http://www.railwaycodes.org.uk/elrs/elr0.shtm), red numbers are decimal mileage of the ELRs.

Black lines/names are tracks pre-fixed with the ELR they belong to, their track IDs/codes (see https://phillipdann-ward.github.io/mapbox/trackcodes.html), and their unqiue NR track asset IDs in brackets.
